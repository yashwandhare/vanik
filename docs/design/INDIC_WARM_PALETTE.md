# VANIK Indic Mandi & Festive Kirana Palette

**Design System Update:** Indic Warm Palette  
**Target:** Festive Indian Kirana & Wholesale Mandi Commerce  
**Branch:** `devc`  

---

## 1. Design Rationale

The previous visual identity relied on cooler European tones: pale greenish cream (`#f8f9eb`), sage gray (`#c0c2a9`), cold forest green (`#003d21`), and pastel mint/lilac/sky washes.

The new **Indic Mandi & Festive Kirana Palette** grounds VANIK in authentic, warm, tactile Indian commerce — invoking the tones of clay diyas, earthen spice sacks, marigold garlands, sandalwood paste, and kora silk:

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  Desi Khadi & Sandalwood Canvas: #fbf7ee                                │
│  Warm Obsidian Charcoal: #1c1917                                       │
│  Royal Terracotta / Gerua: #85290c / #7c2d12                           │
│  Festive Marigold / Haldi Gold: #f59e0b / #fde68a / #fef3c7            │
│  Mandi Cardamom & Tulsi Green: #14532d / #166534 / #dcfce7             │
│  Warm Peetal / Brass / Sandstone Borders: #d8cbbe                      │
│  Multani Mitti & Champa Fills: #f3ebe0                                 │
│  Sacred Kumkum / Sindoor: #991b1b / #7f1d1d / #fee2e2                  │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

## 3. Surface Mapping

### Overview Hero & 3 Surfaces
- **Hero Section:** Architectural Terracotta Earth (`#7c2d12`) with Marigold Saffron (`#fde68a`) badges and cards.
- **Surface 01 (Marketplace Web App):** Festive Marigold Gold (`#fde68a`) with deep amber (`#78350f`) text.
- **Surface 02 (Agent Passports):** Terracotta Clay Wash (`#fed7aa`) with royal terracotta (`#85290c`) text.
- **Surface 03 (Telegram Mini App):** Warm Kesar Silk Wash (`#fef3c7`) with deep kesar amber (`#92400e`) text.

### Mandi Commodity Ledger & Marketplace
- **Catalogue Active Filters:** Royal Terracotta (`#85290c`) active pill with white text.
- **Commodity Prices:** Warm Terracotta/Bronze INR text (`#85290c`).
- **Verified Sellers:** Cardamom/Tulsi green badge (`#dcfce7` / `#14532d`).
- **Seller Cards:** Warm ivory with sandstone hairlines (`#d8cbbe`) and terracotta action buttons.
