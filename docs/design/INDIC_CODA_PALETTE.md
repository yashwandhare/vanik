## 2. Visual Hierarchy & Surface Mapping

1. **Canvas & Layout**:
   - The entire background uses **Chuna (`#F6EDD9`)**, evoking authentic lime-washed handmade paper.
   - Cards and inputs sit on **Ivory (`#FFFAF0`)** with crisp **Retā (`#CDBB98`)** hairlines.

2. **Hero Feature Band**:
   - Signature dark feature section uses **Mor Pankh (`#0E4A47`)** with **Khadi (`#EDE0C4`)** text and **Neem/Genda (`#BCDFB0` / `#F2C85B`)** accents.

3. **Three Surface Tonal Cards**:
   - **Surface 01 (Marketplace Web App):** **Genda Wash (`#F2C85B`)** with Kajal (`#1E1A1C`) text.
   - **Surface 02 (Agent Passports):** **Jamuni Wash (`#D4C2E6`)** with Mor Pankh (`#0E4A47`) accents.
   - **Surface 03 (Telegram Mini App):** **Peacock Wash (`#A8DDD8`)** with Mor Pankh (`#0E4A47`) text.

4. **Security & Red-Team Audit**:
   - Mitigation badges and prompt-injection alerts use **Sindoor (`#C8472F`)** text over **Gulabi Wash (`#F2B6C4`)**.

5. **Trust & Verification**:
   - Demo-verified badges and cryptographic signature tags use **Neem Wash (`#BCDFB0`)** with **Mor Pankh (`#0E4A47`)** text.
